Privacy, safety & accreditation-supporting controls

Important: a front-end page cannot itself be “100% HIPAA compliant” or guarantee JCI accreditation. Compliance depends on the deployed organization, backend, access control, authentication, audit controls, policies, training, physical safeguards, integrations and formal assessment.
HIPAA-supporting design examples
  • Role-oriented access concepts and PHI masking.
  • Identity verification before final admission.
  • Audit-preview events for material actions.
  • Separate payer/payment workflows from clinical data access.
  • No real PHI is intended for this prototype.
JCI-supporting workflow examples
  • Access/continuity-of-care structure.
  • Patient-flow, transfer and discharge lifecycle.
  • Two-identifier verification at admission.
  • Specialized-unit eligibility and bed compatibility.
  • Role-specific assessments after admission.

Reference anchors used for the prototype: HHS HIPAA Security Rule (45 CFR 164 technical/administrative/physical safeguards) and JCI Hospital Standards, 8th Edition (effective 1 Jan 2025). Organization-specific policies and applicable local regulation still govern implementation.

Fast admission guide

Appointment / planned admission

Select Planned Admission → find patient → confirm request → assign specialty/doctor → check coverage → reserve bed → review → admit.

ED admission

Select From Emergency → load source encounter → confirm admission request → assign receiving specialty/team → payer may remain pending under policy → reserve bed → handover → admit.

Keyboard: Ctrl/Cmd + K opens universal search. Search patients, wards, beds or commands such as “coverage”, “bed”, “review”.
