Prototype compliance boundary

This front-end demonstrates controls that can support HIPAA safeguards and JCI-oriented patient-safety workflows. It is not a certification, accreditation, legal determination, or production clinical decision system. Production deployment requires organizational policies, risk analysis, security controls, validated clinical workflows, training, interfaces, audit review and applicable local regulatory approval.
